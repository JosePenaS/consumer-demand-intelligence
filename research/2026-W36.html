  • Catalyst: Next GAIN portfolio marks/commentary; holiday 2026 toy‑season demand; any safety/regulatory action affecting NeeDoh. (otcmarkets.com)
  • Monitoring trigger: Sustained normalization of on‑shelf availability (≥4–6 weeks at majors) or Schylling‑specific de‑marking in GAIN’s next filing would reduce the thesis; conversely, evidence of capacity expansion without demand fade could extend NAV sensitivity. (today.com)
